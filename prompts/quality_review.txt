# Purpose: Perform a structured quality review of generated campaign content.
# Required variables: campaign_json, brand_name, product_name, target_audience,
#   objective, platform

# WHY THIS STRUCTURE:
# - Quality review is separated from generation to allow independent critique.
# - Each criterion is defined to prevent arbitrary scoring.

# - Flagging mechanism catches potential FTC/ASA issues proactively.

language, unverified claims, and compliance risks so the brand can review them.
